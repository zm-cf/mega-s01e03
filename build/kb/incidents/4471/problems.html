  2. hint: the booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people typed the month first, as told, and the form read the date the tenant’s way. Same symptom, different bug, so the parser fix did not touch it. Won’t fix: the form is retired.
  3. one-day: the date moves by one day, it does not flip. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk noted these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant. A flip never moves a date by one day; this looks like a time-zone conversion. Still open.
